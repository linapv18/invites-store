Questions

Before you begin.

Do we need design or coding skills?+

No. You don’t need to know design or code. You fill in your text, photos and details in simple fields, like a form, and see the real invitation beside you as you go. A five-step guide walks you through it.

How does each family RSVP?+

The invitation shows their names and places. Each guest can say whether they will attend. Try it in the demos; demo replies are not sent or saved.

How does a collection design differ from custom design?+

The collection starts with an existing design you personalize. A custom invitation is designed from scratch for your wedding.

How do we share the invitation?+

The experience is designed around a personalized link for each family. Publishing and sending real invitations will be available at launch.

Can we buy or publish now?+

Not yet. For now, explore the designs and try the demos. Pricing, publication duration, terms and support channels will be announced before ordering opens.

Can an invitation have two languages?+

There are Spanish and English demos. The option to include both languages in one invitation and its commercial scope will be confirmed at launch.
